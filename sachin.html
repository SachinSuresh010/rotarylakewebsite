Sachin V Suresh

An entrepreneur and an engineer, he hails from Kochi where he pursued his schooling and higher studies. After his exploits in the middle east for 5 years in Kuwait and Qatar, he returned to his roots to establish various ventures in various industries including lab equipment, medical and hospital equipment distribution and also in the field of Information Technology.

A charter member of Rotary Cochin Lakeside, he is our 6th and youngest president for the year 2024- 25. He has held various positions in the past including International Service Director, Club Administrator, Club Secretary and Vice President.
